Programs

Find your lane.

Six programs, from a first splash to lifeguard training, each paced for where a swimmer is actually starting from.

Novice Training

For absolute beginners, children and adults alike. Coaches work one-on-one with hesitant swimmers, so this is also where students overcome a fear of water.

Semi-Advance Training

For swimmers with the basics in place: refining stroke technique, breathing and endurance before moving to advanced work.

Advance Training

For confident swimmers ready to sharpen technique, build real speed and train through longer, more demanding sessions.

Competitive Swimmer Training

Complete training support for swimmers preparing to compete: technique, conditioning and race-readiness under close coaching.

First Aid Training

Practical first aid training for water-related emergencies, for swimmers, parents and anyone who wants to be prepared.

Lifesaving & Lifeguard Training

For those training to become lifeguards or rescuers: rescue technique, water safety and emergency response.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply directly.

Do I need any swimming experience to join?

No. Novice Training is designed for people starting from zero, including children and adults.

Can you help someone who is afraid of water?

Yes. Novice Training includes one-on-one attention for hesitant swimmers, at a pace they are comfortable with.

What should I bring to my first class?

Swimwear, a towel and, if you have them, a swim cap and goggles. Ask us on WhatsApp if you are unsure what your batch needs.

Which program is right for me or my child?

Message us on WhatsApp with the swimmer's age and current comfort in water, and we will suggest a program and batch.

How do I apply for admission?

Use the online admission form. It prepares a WhatsApp message to our team with your details, and staff confirm your batch.

What are the fees and how do I pay?

Fees depend on the program, and our team confirms the amount when you apply. Pay by UPI on the Pay Fees page.

How long is a batch, and when should I arrive?

Each batch is 40 minutes. Be present 10 minutes before your batch time for free-hand exercise.

Do you offer First Aid and Lifeguard training?

Yes. See First Aid Training and Lifesaving & Lifeguard Training under Programs.

What are your pool timings?

Morning batches start at 6:00, 6:40 and 7:20 AM, evening batches at 4:00, 4:40 and 5:20 PM, and the night batch at 8:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays.
